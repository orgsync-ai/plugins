# Optional ways of working, version 1

Read these only when the person wants a lightweight example for sales and marketing.
They are editable starting points, outside company knowledge and its index.
None is adopted in advance. A company can keep its existing practice, write its own or skip adoption.
If deliberately selected, use `area: "sales_marketing"` and `classification: "way_of_working"` in the reviewed draft or correction.
Agree the exact text and metadata before sharing. Only authorized acceptance of a current, ready, active source makes it adopted.

## Qualify a lead before booking a sales call

Before offering a sales call, ask what the prospect wants to solve, who uses the result and when they need it.
Record what is known and what still needs an answer. Book the call when both sides agree it could help.
Tailor the questions and qualification criteria to the company's customers.

## Review customer conversations weekly

Once a week, review the customer conversations the team chose to record.
Separate observed customer words and actions from the team's interpretation.
Agree one change to test, who will try it and what evidence to revisit at the next review.

## Follow up within two working days

After a customer conversation, send the agreed next step within two working days when the customer permits follow-up.
Name who owns it and record the customer's preferred contact method.
Tailor timing and wording to the company's capacity and the customer's location and preferences.

The person carries out external work. These examples authorize no outreach, purchases or other external actions.
